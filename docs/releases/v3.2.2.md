# v3.2.2 — Safe listening on affected Apple OS versions

Some readers saw Readr disappear a few minutes into Listen, just as Readr Voice
finished downloading and began speaking. Apple's BNNS runtime can terminate the
entire process during Kokoro inference on affected OS versions, so Readr now
keeps that engine off, starts with the Apple voice, and explains why in the
voice menu.

## Fixed

- **Readr no longer quits a few minutes into Listen on crash-prone OS builds.**
  Apple's BNNS runtime can terminate the process when Readr Voice synthesizes
  speech on iOS/iPadOS 26.4+ (including unverified later major versions) and
  macOS 26.4–26.5. Readr Voice is unavailable on those versions, its 104MB
  model is not downloaded, and the Apple voice reads instead. A stored Readr
  Voice preference is preserved for a future verified-safe OS version.

## What to test

**iPhone or iPad on 26.4 or later.** Open an English book and press Listen. An
Apple voice should read from the first sentence. The voice menu should explain
why Readr Voice is unavailable, show no Readr Voice row or "downloading" line,
and listening for at least 10 minutes should not crash. This also confirms the
104MB model was never requested.

**Mac on 26.6 or later.** Open an English book. Readr Voice should remain the
first voice row and the default narrator.

**Stored Apple voice.** Explicitly select an Apple voice, reopen the book, and
open the voice menu. The selected voice should remain active and the
unavailability note should not appear.
