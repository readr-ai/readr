# v3.2.2 — Recap one tap away, a book to start on, and no more quitting mid-Listen

Three things, one release. Readers on iPhone and iPad saw Readr disappear a
few minutes into Listen — exactly when the Readr Voice model finished its
first-use download and began speaking. Product Hunt copy promised a
spoiler-free recap the app could not yet give. And App Review met an empty
library with nothing to put in it.

## What to test

**iPhone or iPad on iOS 26.4 or later.** Fresh install: Alice's Adventures in
Wonderland is already in the library. Open it and press Listen. An Apple voice
reads from the first sentence, and keeps reading for ten minutes without the
app quitting. The voice menu shows a note explaining that Readr Voice is
unavailable on this version of iOS, no Readr Voice row, and no "downloading"
line — the ~104MB model is never requested.

**Mac on macOS 26.6 or later.** Open an English book. Readr Voice is still the
first row of the voice menu and still the default narrator.

**Stored Apple voice.** Explicitly pick an Apple voice, reopen the book, open
the voice menu: the chosen voice is checked and the unavailability note does
not appear.

**Recap, in the reader.** Read a few chapters of Alice. Next to Listen and
Ask there is a Recap button (⇧⌘R on a Mac). Press it: the Ask panel opens
with "Recap what I've read so far — no spoilers" already sent, a "Recap up to
where you are" line above it reading like "Chapter 7 of 12 · 31% · Advice
from a Caterpillar", and the answer streaming in. It names nothing from later
chapters; ask "what happens next?" and Readr declines to say. Press Done,
press Ask: a plain Ask, nothing pre-sent, with the recap as the first chip.

**Whole-book switch.** In that Ask panel the header reads "Ask about what
you've read so far" over the same "Chapter 7 of 12 · 31% · …" line, and the
chips are worded "so far" ("Summarize what I've read so far"). Flip the
"Up to where I am" switch to "Whole book": the where-am-I line goes, the
chips become "Summarize this book" / "What are the key themes?" / "Who are
the main characters?", and the next question may draw on the whole book —
answers already on screen keep the scope they were asked under. Select a
passage and press Ask: the switch is still there, and the second chip reads
"How does this connect to what I've read so far?" (or "…the rest of the
book?" once flipped). Open a PDF shown as original pages: no switch at all.
Ask about a passage on the page you are looking at: it is never treated as
unread.

**Recap, from Home.** Each Continue Reading card for a text book shows
"Chapter N of M" under the Continue pill and a ✦ Recap link. Tap it: the book
opens at your place and a beat later the recap is already sent. PDF cards
show neither, and cards in a row stay level either way. On a Mac with the
reader window already open behind the library, the existing window comes
forward rather than a second one opening.

**Native PDF.** Open a PDF shown as original pages: no Recap button, no
recap chip (a PDF page is not a reading position). Switch to the reading
view in Appearance: the button appears.

**No provider.** Disconnect every provider, press Recap: the panel opens on
"No AI provider connected". Connect a key from there and press Done: the
recap sends itself.

**Sample book.** Delete the app, reinstall, launch: one book in the library,
which opens and paginates, and is a book like any other (its source is
retained, it has an added-on date, it appears in Recently Added). Import
another book, delete Alice, relaunch: Alice does not come back. Delete every
book, relaunch: still an empty library — the seed fires only into a library
that has never been written to disk, never into one you emptied.

## Fixed

- **Readr no longer quits a few minutes into Listen on iOS 26.** Kokoro
  inference on iOS/iPadOS 26.4+ (and macOS 26.4–26.5) can terminate the
  process inside Apple's BNNS runtime (FluidAudio#817/#844) — uncatchable,
  and no compute-unit routing avoids it. On those builds Readr Voice is now
  disabled at the engine: it is not offered, not defaulted to, and its model
  is not downloaded; Apple's voices read instead and the voice menu says why.
  A stored Readr Voice preference is kept, so it returns the day the OS is
  verified safe. iOS is gated fail-closed (26.4 through every later version)
  until a device build passes; macOS 26.6+ is verified fixed and stays on.

## New

- **Recap, one tap away, and spoiler-free.** A Recap button in the reader
  toolbar (iPhone, iPad and Mac, ⇧⌘R) and a ✦ Recap action on each Continue
  Reading card open Ask with the recap already sent, under a line saying
  where it stops: "Chapter 7 of 12 · 31% · Advice from a Caterpillar". Only
  text books get it; a native PDF page is not a reading position.
- **Ask sees only what you have read — unless you say otherwise.**
  Every question from a text book is scoped to your reading position: the
  whole-book tier sends only the text before it, the retrieval tier asks the
  index for passages at or before it (filtered before the limit, so a scoped
  question still gets a full set) and drops anything past it before it can be
  quoted or cited, and the prompt defines a recap as "so far, in reading
  order, stopping where the reader stopped" and forbids drawing on what the
  model knows of the book from elsewhere. The position never sits behind the
  page in front of you: a selected passage is read by definition, and in the
  scroll layout the whole current chapter counts as read. A **Whole book**
  switch in the panel header lifts the scope for the questions that follow;
  the chips say "so far" while it is off, and PDFs shown as original pages —
  no reading position — get neither the switch nor the scope. Nothing has
  been read yet? No book text is sent, and the recap says there is nothing
  to recap.
- **A book to start on.** A fresh install seeds the library with Alice's
  Adventures in Wonderland (public domain, 66KB, twelve chapters), through
  the same import path a file you open takes. The seed fires only into a
  library that has never been written to disk — not into one you emptied —
  and the "seeded" flag is set only when a book was actually seeded, so it
  never reappears after you delete it. App Review notes now say so — the
  previous "ships with no books" lines were true of 3.2.1 and false of this
  build.

## Under the hood

- `NeuralVoiceAvailability.isCrashProneOS` (ReadrKit, table-tested) is the
  single OS gate; `KokoroSpeechEngine` starts in a new `.unsupported`
  readiness on gated builds and refuses prepare/initialize/speak itself, so
  the router's existing not-ready path does the fallback with no new checks.
- `ReadingScope` (ReadrKit) is the named choice — `.wholeBook` or
  `.upTo(ReadingFrontier)` — with no default at any layer: the strategy,
  `AskService.ask`, the view model's `ask(_:scope:)` and every test name
  theirs. `RAGIndex.retrieve` takes a `maxChapterIndex` applied before the
  limit; `RetrievedPassage` records the chapter it came from.
- `ReadingLengthTable` (ReadrKit) is the one pass over the text — characters
  and words per chapter — cached per book by `ReadingLengthCache` in the app,
  and behind `ReadingPositionSummary`, the "~N min left" estimate and the
  scoped token budget. `Book.chapterTitle` / `chapterDisplayTitle` /
  `tocTitle(forChapterIndex:)` live in ReadrKit with one fallback wording,
  "Chapter N", shared by the reader header, Contents, the caption and the
  prompt anchor (which is built from the summary).
- `AskRequest` carries one presentation of the panel (selection, scope,
  initial question) and drives `.sheet(item:)`, so each opening has a fresh
  identity and the `@StateObject` is created from that request; the caption
  is computed in the panel from the book and the frontier. The Continue
  Reading card's recap is consumed from a `.task(id:)` keyed on the pending
  id and the reader's appearance, with no deferral; a stale
  `pendingRecapBookID` is cleared by any reader that appears for a different
  book, by a reader window that cannot show its book, and by an `open` that
  cannot open.
- `AppModel.init(store:parsers:seedsSampleBook:)`: the entry point decides
  from the launch arguments; tests and previews pass `false`. Seeding runs
  the app's one import path (`ingest`), and `LibraryStore.hasPersistedLibrary`
  gates it. An app-hosted test in the snapshot target fails CI if the bundled
  EPUB ever falls out of the app bundle.
- Store copy no longer leads with Readr Voice on iOS; the promotional text
  and description describe Listen with Apple's voices and the recap.
