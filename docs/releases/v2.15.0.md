# v2.15.0 — Ask keeps the conversation; settings and shelves read straight

An Ask-and-settings release. Ask stopped being a one-shot question box: it
holds a conversation, shows your question as sent, and renders answers as
formatted text instead of the Markdown punctuation the model wrote. Provider
settings now has one card per company rather than one per way of connecting.
Plus the shelf-alignment and footnote-marker fixes, and the first macOS release
that installs by dragging.

This is also the first build shaped for App Store submission: the two paths
that were App Review liabilities are gone from the iOS build (and untouched on
the direct-download Mac build).

## What to test

**Ask a follow-up.** Open a book, select a sentence, ask something — then ask
again without closing the panel. Each question should appear immediately as a
sent bubble and stay in the transcript; the field should empty as you send. The
second answer should understand the first: "but roads are technically 3D" after
a question about roads should read as an objection, not as a fresh question.
Answers should come back *formatted* — bold as bold, quotes as an indented rule
— with no `**` or `>` visible, and shorter than before.

**Home shelves.** Look down the Continue Reading and Recently Added rows. Every
card's title, author, progress bar, percentage and Continue button should sit
on the same line as its neighbour's, whatever the titles do, and the covers
should share one left edge.

**Footnote markers.** Open a book with numbered notes. The markers in the body
text should be raised and small, not full-size numbers sitting in the line.
Already-imported books keep their old layout until re-imported.

**AI Providers.** One card per company. OpenAI's card offers the subscription
and the API key as two doors into the same account (macOS; iOS shows the key
path only). Every action should look tappable, and a disconnected card should
tell you what to do.

**macOS install.** Download the DMG from the release, mount it, drag Readr to
Applications. Gatekeeper should not complain, offline included.

## New

- **Ask is a conversation.** The panel is a transcript over a pinned composer.
  Earlier turns ride along with each new question — bounded to the most recent
  ones, each abridged, so the chat can't crowd the book out of the context
  window. Retry re-runs the failed turn instead of stacking a duplicate.

- **Answers render as text, not Markdown source.** Paragraphs, headings,
  quotes, lists, code and rules are laid out in the panel's own style; inline
  bold/italic/links come from the platform parser. The parser tolerates
  half-written input, so a partial answer formats as far as it has streamed.

- **Provider settings group by company.** `ChatGPT (subscription)` and
  `OpenAI (API key)` were two sibling cards for the same account, which read as
  two separate services. They share one card now; the underlying providers stay
  separately connected, validated and activated.

- **macOS ships a drag-to-Applications DMG** alongside the zip, notarized and
  stapled so Gatekeeper clears it offline.

## Fixed

- **Shelf cards didn't line up.** Two separate drifts. The caption under each
  jacket sized itself to its own text, so a title that wrapped to two lines —
  or a book with no author — moved that card's hairline, percentage and
  Continue pill relative to its neighbour's. And covers narrower than 2:3 were
  centred in their slot, sitting a few points in from their own card's title
  and from the covers beside them. Captions now reserve a fixed two title lines
  and one author line with the title pinned to the top, and jackets rest on
  their slot's bottom-*leading* corner. Pinned by rendered-pixel tests.

- **Note markers sat inline at full size.** EPUB 3 producers often mark a
  reference semantically (`epub:type="noteref"`) and leave the raising to the
  reading system, shipping no `<sup>` and no `vertical-align` — so
  "…right there.123" rendered as body text. Those are raised now, keeping their
  tap target. Bibliography references are deliberately left on the baseline,
  where convention puts them.

- **Answers were too verbose.** The prompt now asks for the answer first, two
  or three short paragraphs at most, one quotation at most, and sparing
  Markdown.

- **Nothing showed where to click in provider settings.** Sign in, Get an API
  key and Check again were bare captions in the same grey as the explanatory
  text beside them. They wear filled or bordered pills now, and each
  disconnected card opens with a line saying what to do.

## Under the hood

- Two new ReadrKit types with full test coverage: `AnswerMarkdown` (block
  structure for streamed answers) and `ProviderVendor` (grouping connection
  methods by company, deriving each card's badge from the methods a build
  actually exposes).
- `ContextStrategy` takes conversation history, bounded and abridged; a
  convenience overload keeps the one-shot call.
- A finalize-time backstop collapses nested baseline shifts during EPUB
  extraction — two superscript spans over one run used to compound the
  renderer's per-span shrink.

## Store-submission notes

Removed from the **iOS build only**, both kept on the direct-download Mac
build:

- The **ChatGPT subscription card**, which rides an unofficial backend
  (docs/AUTH.md ToS caveat). iOS keeps the fully sanctioned paths: OpenRouter
  sign-in and Anthropic/OpenAI keys. A beta user who already connected ChatGPT
  keeps a working provider — credentials and routing are untouched, only the
  card is hidden.
- The **"Get an API key" console links**, which point at pages selling API
  credits (Guideline 3.1.1, purchases outside IAP).

Both absences are asserted by the iPhone and iPad UI-test lanes, so they are
regression tests for the store gate rather than adjusted expectations.
