# v3.1.0 — Listen, fixed; and a voice worth listening to

v3.0.0 shipped narration; real listening found what the simulator hadn't. This
release fixes every reported breaker — the repeating sentences, the jumping,
the footnote numbers read out loud — and adds **Readr Voice (Beta)**: the
Kokoro-82M neural narrator, on-device, chosen over the platform voices by A/B
listening. It stays true to the zero-egress promise: the voice runs entirely on
the device; the one download is the model itself, fetched once from a fixed
CDN when the voice is first picked (~104MB), never the book's text.

## Fixed

- **The repeats.** Leaving the reader with the notes panel open left narration
  running invisibly; reopening the book stacked a second voice a few sentences
  behind the first — heard as "it repeats the same sentences". Narration now
  always dies with its reader, and the lock screen is cleaned up with it.
- **The jumping.** A late completion callback from a replaced utterance could
  silently "finish" sentences the voice never spoke, racing ahead; callbacks
  are now matched to their exact utterance. An audio interruption (a call,
  Siri) no longer machine-guns silently through pages — narration holds as
  paused and resumes in place. Pausing in the same instant a sentence ends no
  longer un-pauses itself.
- **Footnote markers.** Noteref digits are muted from speech (and the sentence
  breaks they jammed are restored) — while the ₂ of CO₂ and the ² of mc² stay
  spoken. Read-along positions are unaffected; the muting is offset-exact.

## New — Readr Voice (Beta)

Top of the voice picker for English books, strictly opt-in, never the default.
Picking it starts the model download immediately; the Apple voices remain the
zero-download default and the store copy stays honest. Sentence controls,
speed, sleep timer, page-follow — everything rides the same narration engine.

Known limits, tracked in ROADMAP M10: read-along follows per sentence (word
timings are next); the download has no progress UI yet; FluidAudio reports an
intermittent synthesis crash on current iOS (FluidAudio#844) — beta stays
opt-in until it's cleared upstream.

## Platform note

The macOS app is **Apple-silicon-only from this release**: the neural voice
stack uses the Neural Engine and won't compile for or run on Intel Macs.
Simulator builds are likewise arm64-only.

## What to test

**The ghost is dead.** Start listening, open the notes panel, navigate back to
the library. The voice must stop the moment you leave. Reopen the book and
listen again: exactly one voice.

**Interruptions.** Get a phone call (or invoke Siri) mid-sentence. Narration
should hold as paused, and play should resume the same sentence — not skip
ahead, not sit on a fake "playing".

**Readr Voice.** Pick "Readr Voice (Beta)" from the voice menu mid-sentence.
The sentence should re-speak in the new voice once the model is in (first pick
downloads ~104MB — music should keep playing during the download). Speed
changes, sentence skips, chapter crossings, and the sleep timer should behave
identically to the platform voices.

**Footnotes.** In a book with noterefs, the voice should read straight through
"…the war ended.¹² Peace came…" as two clean sentences, no "twelve" — while a
chemistry text still says its formulas.
