# v3.2.0 — Readr Voice by default, with nothing to wait for

v3.1.0 shipped the neural narrator as an opt-in row at the top of a menu.
Testing it on a real phone made the problem obvious: nobody finds a better
voice behind a picker, and a ~104MB download you have to sit through is a
download flow, not a listening experience.

So v3.2.0 flips both:

- **Readr Voice is the default narrator for English books.** No picking, no
  setting — press Listen and this is the voice you get. An explicitly chosen
  Apple voice is still honoured, and non-English books keep the platform
  voices.
- **Narration never waits on the download.** Listen starts speaking instantly
  with the Apple voice; the model downloads in the background; the voice
  switches over automatically at a sentence boundary the moment it's ready.
  The voice menu says what's happening ("Readr Voice is downloading —
  switching automatically when ready") and offers a retry if the download
  fails. Your music keeps playing during the download — the audio session is
  only claimed when there's audio to play.

The "(Beta)" tag is gone from the name. The upstream iOS synthesis crash we
were tracking (FluidAudio#844) was closed by the maintainers; we keep watching
it, and if a synthesis ever fails outright the platform voice is the built-in
fallback.

## What to test

**Fresh install, first Listen.** Delete the app, reinstall, open an English
book, press Listen. The voice should start immediately (Apple voice), the
voice menu should show the downloading note with "Readr Voice" checked, and
within a couple of minutes on Wi-Fi the narration should audibly change to the
neural voice mid-chapter — without a hiccup in position.

**Explicit choice still wins.** Pick Samantha explicitly, kill the app,
reopen: still Samantha. Pick Readr Voice again: it switches back.

**Airplane mode.** Fresh install, airplane mode on, press Listen. The Apple
voice reads normally; the menu shows the failed-download note; nothing stalls.
Disable airplane mode, re-pick Readr Voice: the download resumes.
