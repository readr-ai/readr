Readr 3.2: a voice worth listening to.

  entirely on your device, now the default for English books
• Nothing to wait for: listening starts instantly with an Apple voice while
  the voice model downloads once (~104MB), then switches over automatically

  after a phone call
• Your book never leaves the device to be read aloud; Apple's zero-download
