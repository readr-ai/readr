READ IT, OR LISTEN TO IT

Press Listen and Readr reads your book aloud, starting from the page you're
on. The page turns itself to follow the voice, so you can put the phone in
your pocket and pick the book back up later — reading or listening — exactly
where you stopped.

• Sentence and chapter skip, speed, and a choice of voice
• Change speed or voice mid-sentence and it carries on from that word
• A sleep timer that counts listening, not clock time — pause for lunch and
  the countdown waits for you
• Keeps playing with the screen locked, with lock-screen and headphone
  controls
• Works on EPUBs and PDFs alike

The voice is Apple's, running on your device. No account, no subscription,
nothing to download — and nothing about your book leaves the device to be
read aloud.
