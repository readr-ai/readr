Readr 3.2.1 makes listening better.

• English books can now use Readr Voice, a more natural narrator that reads
  on your device
• Listening starts with an Apple voice while the one-time Readr Voice
  download finishes (about 104MB), then switches over automatically
• Footnote numbers are no longer read aloud (formulas still are — that
  one's next)
• Fixed: repeated sentences, narration jumping ahead, and playback wedging
  after a phone call
• Apple's built-in voices are still one tap away, and are used for books in
  other languages
