Readr 3.2: a voice worth listening to.

• Meet Readr Voice — a natural neural narrator (Kokoro-82M) that runs
  entirely on your device, now the default for English books
• Nothing to wait for: listening starts instantly with an Apple voice while
  the voice model downloads once (~104MB), then switches over automatically
• Footnote numbers are no longer read aloud (formulas still are — that
  one's next)
• Fixed: repeated sentences, narration jumping ahead, and playback wedging
  after a phone call
• Your book never leaves the device to be read aloud; Apple's built-in
  voices remain one tap away
