Readr 3.3.0: Readr Voice is back on iPhone and iPad.

• Readr Voice — our natural neural narrator that runs entirely on your
  device — reads English books again on iPhone and iPad, on a new engine
  that avoids the iOS issue behind 3.2.2's pause. Listening starts instantly
  with an Apple voice while the voice model downloads once, then switches
  over on its own; with the screen locked an Apple voice reads and Readr
  Voice returns when you come back
• Tap Recap — in the reader, or on a Continue Reading card — and Readr tells
  you what's happened so far, from only the pages you've read. Nothing ahead
  of your place is used, so it can't spoil what comes next
• A first book in your library: Alice's Adventures in Wonderland is there on
  first launch, so you can try reading, listening and asking right away
• Your book never leaves the device to be read aloud; Apple's zero-download
  voices remain one tap away
