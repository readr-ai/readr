Readr is an ebook reader you can talk to, and listen to.

Open an EPUB or PDF you already own, select a sentence, and ask about it.
Readr answers with the whole book as context — and it isn't limited to the
book: ask how the science has moved on, who directed the adaptation, or how
an idea aged, and you get a real answer, with a clear signal for which part
came from the book and which from beyond it. What it will never do is invent
what the book says.

Highlight as you read, then turn those highlights into a first draft. Readr
orders them by reading position, keeps your quotes intact, and composes an
editable article you can export as Markdown.

LISTEN TO IT
Press Listen and Readr reads the book aloud, starting from the page you're on
— not the top of the chapter. The page turns itself to follow the voice, and
the place it saves is where the voice left off, so switching between reading
and listening never costs you your spot.

Skip by sentence or by chapter, change the speed or the voice mid-sentence and
it carries on from that word, and set a sleep timer that counts listening
rather than clock time — pause for lunch and the countdown waits for you. It
keeps playing with the screen locked, with lock-screen and headphone controls,
and it works on PDFs as well as EPUBs.

The narrator for English books is Readr Voice, a neural voice that runs
entirely on your device: no account, no subscription, and nothing about your
book ever leaves the device to be read aloud. Books in other languages are
read by Apple's voices. Its voice model downloads once (about 104MB) the
first time you listen; until it's in, an Apple voice reads and the switch
happens mid-chapter on its own. Prefer Apple's built-in voices? They're one
tap away in the voice menu, with nothing to download at all.

BRING YOUR OWN AI
Readr has no subscription and no account. Connect your own Anthropic, OpenAI
or OpenRouter key, or sign in with OpenRouter — you pay your provider
directly, at cost, and you can change or disconnect at any time. Without a
provider Readr is still a complete reader; the AI features simply sit idle.

PRIVATE BY CONSTRUCTION
No telemetry. No analytics. No account. Your books, highlights, notes and
questions live on your device, and reading them aloud happens there too. A
question leaves it only when you choose to ask a cloud model, and it goes
straight to your provider — never through a server of ours, because we don't
run one. API keys are stored in the device Keychain.

READING THAT FEELS LIKE READING
• EPUB, PDF, plain-text and Markdown — DRM-free files you own
• Three layouts: continuous scroll, single page, or two facing pages
• Paper, Sepia and Dark themes, with your choice of typeface and spacing
• Table of contents, bookmarks, in-book search, footnote popups
• Five highlight colors, notes, and a Notes panel that exports to Markdown
• Continue Reading picks up exactly where you left off, reading or listening
• On iPad: sidebar and reader side by side, two-page spreads, and hardware
  keyboard page turns

OPEN SOURCE
Readr is MIT-licensed and developed in the open. Read the code, file an
issue, or send a bug report from inside the app — there's a Report a bug
button in Settings that attaches your app version and recent errors, and
nothing about what you've been reading.
